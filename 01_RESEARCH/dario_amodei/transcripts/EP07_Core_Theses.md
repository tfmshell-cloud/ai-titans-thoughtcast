# Dario Amodei — Core Architectural & Philosophical Theses (EP07)

1. **Scaling Laws (2020):** Empirical predictability of emergent intelligence across 7+ orders of magnitude of compute, data, and parameters — turning deep learning from alchemy into statistical physics.
2. **Constitutional AI (RLAIF, 2022):** Principle-based alignment over opaque human censorship, training models to critique and revise their own outputs against an explicit, inspectable constitution.
3. **Mechanistic Interpretability (2024):** Opening the neural black box — using dictionary learning and sparse autoencoders (monosemantic features) to map the computational "neurobiology" inside frontier transformers like Claude 3 Sonnet.
4. **Machines of Loving Grace (Oct 2024):** The "compressed 21st century" hypothesis — compressing 50–100 years of biological and medical progress into 5–10 years, eliminating infectious disease, halving cancer mortality, and healing neuropsychiatric disorders.
5. **Public Benefit Governance & Responsible Scaling (PBC / ASL-1..4):** Structuring Anthropic as a Public Benefit Corporation with Long-Term Benefit Trust oversight, paired with quantitative capability thresholds (AI Safety Levels) modeled after BSL-1..4 biosafety labs.

## Key Verified Biographical & Timeline Anchors
- **1983:** Born in San Francisco, California to Riccardo Amodei (Tuscan leather craftsman) and Elena Engel (project manager for libraries).
- **2006:** Father Riccardo passes away from a rare disease; BS in Physics at Stanford University (after beginning undergraduate studies at Caltech).
- **2011:** PhD in Biophysics at Princeton University (Hertz Fellow; dissertation on network-scale multi-electrode electrophysiology of neural circuits in the retina).
- **2014–2016:** Deep Speech 2 researcher at Baidu; Staff Research Scientist at Google Brain (pioneered early work on Concrete Problems in AI Safety).
- **2016–2020:** VP of Research at OpenAI; co-authored empirical Scaling Laws (`arXiv:2001.08361`), led RLHF foundations, and oversaw GPT-2 / GPT-3 architectures.
- **Late 2020 – Early 2021:** Quietly departed OpenAI with sister Daniela Amodei and core safety/scaling researchers; incorporated Anthropic as a Public Benefit Corporation (named partially in homage to information theory pioneer Claude Shannon).
- **July 25, 2023:** Testified before the US Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Privacy, Technology, and the Law regarding CBRN biological weapons risks and compute thresholds.
- **2024:** Released Claude 3 family, *Scaling Monosemanticity* interpretability breakthrough, and the landmark manifesto *Machines of Loving Grace*.
