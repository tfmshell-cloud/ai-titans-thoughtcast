# Andrew Ng — Core Architectural & Educational Theses

1. **AI is the New Electricity:** Just as electricity transformed agriculture, manufacturing, transport, and healthcare 100 years ago, AI acts as a general-purpose infrastructure reshaping every sector of society.
2. **Democratization of Education (Stanford CS229 -> Coursera -> DeepLearning.AI):** Removing gatekeeping walls so millions of builders from Kyiv to Lagos can master modern machine learning from first principles.
3. **Google Brain & Unsupervised Scale (2011–2012):** Linking 1,000 commodity servers (16,000 CPU cores) with Jeff Dean to discover the famous high-level cat neuron from unlabeled YouTube video streams.
4. **Data-Centric AI:** Shifting engineering discipline from endlessly tweaking model hyperparameters toward systematically engineering clean, consistently labeled datasets for real-world factories and hospitals.
5. **Agentic Workflows & Open-Weight Democracy:** Empowering iterative self-reflection loops where small open models outthink monolithic single-pass prompts, plus incubating pragmatic real-world startups through AI Fund and Landing AI.
