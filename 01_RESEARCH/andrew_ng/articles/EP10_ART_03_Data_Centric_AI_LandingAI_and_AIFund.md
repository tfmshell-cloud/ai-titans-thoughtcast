Questions

, answered

How is ADE different from OCR + LLM approaches?

Teams using generic LLMs often struggle with hallucinations and missing source attribution across PDFs and spreadsheets. ADE is vision-first, extracting structured JSON with full grounding. It preserves layout and structure—tables, forms, and headings—delivering higher accuracy, fewer brittle heuristics, and stronger governance for production.

What document types does ADE support?

ADE supports a wide range of document types including PDFs, images (JPEG, PNG, TIFF), scanned documents, spreadsheets, invoices, receipts, contracts, medical records, and more. Our vision-first approach means we handle complex layouts, multi-column documents, and mixed content seamlessly.

What about data privacy and security?

Security is core to our platform. LandingAI is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR &amp; HIPAA compliant. We offer zero data retention options and flexible deployment models including cloud, on-premises, and virtual private deployments to meet your organization&#x27;s security requirements.

How does pricing work?

Our pricing is based on document volume and the features you need. We offer flexible plans for teams of all sizes, from startups to enterprise. Contact our sales team for a customized quote tailored to your specific use case and processing needs.
