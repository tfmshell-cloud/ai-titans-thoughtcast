# Yoshua Bengio — Core Architectural & Philosophical Theses

1. **Academic Independence:** Refusing Silicon Valley corporate offers to defend open scientific inquiry and moral freedom from Fort Mount Royal.
2. **Distributed Representations & Word Embeddings (2000–2003):** Turning isolated symbolic words into continuous coordinates in semantic space (fighting the curse of dimensionality).
3. **Attention Mechanism (Bahdanau, Cho & Bengio, 2014):** Overcoming fixed-length seq2seq bottlenecks via soft dynamic alignment — the mathematical cornerstone of modern Transformers.
4. **Mila & Open Family Laboratory Culture:** Building the world's largest independent academic AI hub in Montreal without secrecy or internal competition, focused on climate, medicine, and public good.
5. **From System 1 to System 2, GFlowNets & Scientist AI:** Moving beyond overconfident associative chatbots toward causal world understanding, calibrated epistemic humility, and probabilistic search (`GFlowNets`).
6. **Montreal Declaration & International AI Safety Diplomacy:** Championing democratic governance, human dignity, non-agentic safeguards, and the UN-backed International Scientific Report on Advanced AI Safety.
