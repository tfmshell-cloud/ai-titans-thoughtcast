# Fei-Fei Li — Core Architectural & Philosophical Theses (EP08)

1. **Data-Centric AI (ImageNet, 2006–2009):** Algorithms starve in a dark room; evolution gave living minds 540 million years of continuous visual data since the Cambrian Explosion. Scaling clean real-world datasets unlocks true intelligence.
2. **The Double Life (Grit of Ormont Cleaners):** Mondays through Fridays studying quantum physics at Princeton; Fridays 4 PM through Sunday nights ironing shirts and translating customer calls in the family's struggling Parsippany, NJ dry cleaner.
3. **Human-Centered AI (Stanford HAI & Ambient Healthcare):** Technology has no independent values; centered on human dignity, medical empathy (privacy-preserving hospital depth sensors), and protecting patients and clinicians.
4. **Spatial Intelligence (World Labs, 2024):** Moving beyond flat 2D text tokens and pixels into interactive 3D physical worlds governed by geometry, mass, gravity, and action.
5. **Inclusion & The North Star (AI4ALL):** Never confuse humble origins with small destiny; go where ego keeps others from getting their hands dirty, and widen the doors of AI through AI4ALL.
