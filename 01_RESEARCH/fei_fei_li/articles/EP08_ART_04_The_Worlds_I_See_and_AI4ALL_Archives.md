# About AI4ALL


AI4ALL graduates are problem-solving at the cutting edge.


### Positioning students to excel in the AI-driven economy


AI4ALL is an award-winning national nonprofit that elevates promising undergraduate talent into impactful AI roles. Graduates of our Ignite Accelerator gain skills in critical thinking and relationship-building, and expertise in human-centered AI. Their work spans brain tumor detection, Alzheimer’s prediction, deep fake detection, eye tracking for accessible scrolling, air quality detection—and more.
