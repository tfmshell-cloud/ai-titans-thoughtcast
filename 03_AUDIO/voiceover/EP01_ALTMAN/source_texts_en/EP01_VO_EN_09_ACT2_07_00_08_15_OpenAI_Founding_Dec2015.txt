On December 11, 2015, OpenAI was unveiled to the world: a non-profit research laboratory armed with one billion dollars in pledged commitments. Its charter: to build Artificial General Intelligence that benefits all of humanity.

The first three years were wandering in the dark. Researchers trained robotic hands to solve Rubik's cubes and pitted neural nets against Dota 2 champions. Academia dismissed them as a billionaire vanity project without academic rigor.

Yet inside the lab, Sutskever and Altman's heretical hypothesis was taking root: Scale is all you need.
